## Background

Research included in the 2025 Legal Trends Report shows that clients are using AI to help with their legal problems. For law firms, this means that potential clients might get what they need from ChatGPT before they reach out to a lawyer.

## Approach

This post summarizes key data points from the Legal Trends Report showing how clients are using AI, how their expectations for professional legal services are evolving, and what they can do to reinforce the value of working with a law firm.

> "It's no secret: Clients are consulting AI solutions to get insight into their legal problems, often before contacting an actual lawyer."
