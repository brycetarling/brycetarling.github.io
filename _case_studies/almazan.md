## Background

Customer stories support Clio's mid- to bottom-of-funnel pipeline. They help reinforce value with customer proof points from personalities that lawyers can trust. These stories help support Clio's marketing campaigns and are used by sales staff to follow up with prospects.

## Approach

The narrative for this customer story came out of a video interview that our team filmed with Alex Almazan to support a series of social videos. I worked with the transcripts to repurpose the content into a story that the Design team used to create a shareable PDF for our marketing and sales teams.

> "If you're a young lawyer and you're working at a place that's making you bill 250 hours a month, run," says Almazan. "Go somewhere that allows you to use this technology to actually do the things that I wish I could have done."
