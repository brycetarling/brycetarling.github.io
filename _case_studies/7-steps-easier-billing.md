## Background

Billing and payments are core workflows within the Clio platform. This asset was created to support mid-funnel engagement by encouraging prospects to learn about how to reduce friction in what is typically a complicated process for billing clients at a law firm.

## Approach

I worked with the Sales team to identify key workflows to help focus Clio's lead generation. Billing was overwhelmingly integral to the majority of closed-won sales conversations at the time. I developed the content to reflect key selling points of the platform.

> "The key to improving any system is to consider all of the touchpoints that can bog down workflows and create room for error."
