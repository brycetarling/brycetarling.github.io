## Background

Featuring aggregated and anonymized data from tens of thousands of legal professionals, Clio's online database provides hourly rate benchmarks for individual states and practice areas. The resource provides insight into how lawyers set their rates, as well as other business performance insights to help manage revenues.

## Approach

Clio's SEO team identified a high volume of search intent for determining hourly rates in specific states and practice areas. I worked with the SEO, Design, and Dev teams to create repeatable page templates that could incorporate structured data pulls.

We created over 50 pages with thousands of structured data points to inform search and AI-related responses to user queries.
