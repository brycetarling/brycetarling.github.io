## Background

Every year, Clio conducts extensive research to better understand some of the most important business trends within the legal industry. This year's report provides insight into AI adoption in law firms, consumer trends, and a first-of-its-kind neurological study into how legal professionals can reduce cognitive load in their work.

## Approach

Working closely with Clio's team of researchers, I help shape the overall narrative and approach to the report to surface insights relevant to an audience of legal professionals, while adhering to Clio's high standards for branding and communication. This includes writing content, developing data visualizations, researching supporting data, and helping manage production and timelines.

> "Legal professionals today have access to technologies that weren't even dreamed of a generation ago. With these innovations, firms have the ability to redefine legal practice as we know it."
