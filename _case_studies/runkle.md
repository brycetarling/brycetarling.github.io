## Background

When Clio launched the ability to create and manage Google's Local Services Ads, Joe Runkle provided the ideal use case for building a steady pipeline of clients for his law firm.

## Approach

Working with the customer marketing team and product teams, I wrote Joe Runkle's customer story to highlight the rapid growth in business for his law firm.

> "I spent $12,000 from January to June, and my profit during those six months was over $100,000. The amount of return I'm seeing is insane. It is by far the best marketing that I have ever done or seen."
