## Background

When Danelle Harvey-Jacob first started her law firm, she gave herself a budget of $1,000 to put everything in place, including her technology. With Clio, she was able to implement a practice management system and CRM, complete with a website and automations to handle everyday administrative work.

## Approach

I worked with the customer marketing team to interview Danelle and uncover key insights into how she was using Clio's products to save time at her law firm.

> "The key to her success? A deep understanding of technology\u2014and how it can accelerate a business's work in achieving its potential."
