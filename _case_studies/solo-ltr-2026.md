## Background

Based on research published in the Legal Trends Report, I worked with Clio's research team to pull research data segmented specifically for an audience of solo and small law firms. This data gives us the ability to compare trends to larger firms within the legal market.

## Approach

As an extension to the main Legal Trends Report published each fall, this report maintains a similar style and branding to reinforce continuity between the overarching themes and narratives driven by the research. I worked with our Demand Generation team to market this resource to a small-firm audiences as a lead gen asset.

> "Lawyers are doing higher-quality work, feeling less stressed, and reclaiming time that was once lost to administrative tasks. But most smaller firms are still trying to figure out how to turn those gains into business growth."
