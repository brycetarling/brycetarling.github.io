## Background

Writing content for executive leaders requires understanding the key areas of their expertise, the nuances in how they communicate, and the forum in which they will be sharing their insights.

## Approach

Ghostwriting requires a lot of forethought and flexibility, since executive team leaders are typically busy and short on time. Being able to steer the direction of a piece of content can help get the gears in motion. At the same time, when the subject is ready to riff, they often provide the most valuable insights worth capturing.
