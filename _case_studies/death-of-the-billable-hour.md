## Background

With AI, lawyers are getting client work done faster. For those billing by the hour, this can mean less revenue for their law firm.

## Approach

This post looks at the latest research and gives insight into how much revenue law firms could be losing with AI if they stick with their traditional billing models. This post also offers guidance on how lawyers can adapt their pricing to take better advantage of AI _and_ boost revenues.

> "The sooner that lawyers adapt to the opportunities presented by AI, the sooner they'll be able to use them to benefit their business."
