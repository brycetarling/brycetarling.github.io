## Background

If firms don't provide access to the tools and resources that their staff need, they'll be more likely to use their own software in their work. For law firms, this can create inherent risks to attorney-client privilege and result in malpractice for the firm.

## Approach

This post describes the risks inherent with the use of shadow IT and what law firms can do to protect themselves. Drawing on the latest research in AI use in law firms, offers preventative strategies for creating clear guidelines and keeping the firm in compliance.
