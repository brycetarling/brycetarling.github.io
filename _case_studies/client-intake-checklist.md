## Background

Checklists and templates were strong lead drivers across multiple marketing campaigns. We designed a campaign built around these assets specifically to educate and drive interest in Clio's client intake tools.

## Approach

We developed a unique brand for a summer education program and launched an ongoing nurture for enrolment. The program featured checklists, templates, and short webinar recordings dripped over several months.
